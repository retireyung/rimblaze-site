Tim Duncan facts
How many career points did Tim Duncan score?

Tim Duncan scored 26,496 career regular-season points in 1,392 games, 19.0 per game, which ranks 20th all-time.

How many triple-doubles did Tim Duncan have?

4 career triple-doubles and 841 double-doubles. The most recent was Dec 5, 2014 against MEM (14 points, 10 rebounds, 10 assists).

What is Tim Duncan’s career high?

53 points on Dec 26, 2001 against DAL.

What was Tim Duncan’s best season?

His best scoring season was 2001-02 with SAS: 25.5 points, 12.7 rebounds and 3.7 assists per game.

Where is Tim Duncan from?

Tim Duncan is from Christiansted, United States Virgin Islands. He played high school ball at St. Dunstan's Episcopal High School in United States Virgin Islands, United States. He played college ball at Wake Forest University.

What teams did Tim Duncan play for?

San Antonio Spurs (1997–2015).
