Quick answers

Kevin Durant facts
How many career points does Kevin Durant have?

Kevin Durant has 32,763 career regular-season points in 1,209 games, 27.1 per game, which ranks 5th all-time.

How many triple-doubles does Kevin Durant have?

18 career triple-doubles and 266 double-doubles. The most recent was Dec 27, 2023 against HOU (27 points, 10 rebounds, 16 assists).

What is Kevin Durant’s career high?

55 points on Apr 2, 2022 at ATL.

What was Kevin Durant’s best season?

His best scoring season was 2013-14 with OKC: 32.0 points, 7.4 rebounds and 5.5 assists per game.

How is Kevin Durant doing this season?

2026-27: 20.8 points, 5.3 rebounds and 3.1 assists in 8 games, with 0 triple-doubles.

Where is Kevin Durant from?

Kevin Durant is from Suitland, Maryland. He played high school ball at Montrose Christian School in United States, then Oak Hill Academy in Mouth of Wilson, Virginia. He played college ball at University of Texas at Austin.

What teams has Kevin Durant played for?

Seattle SuperSonics (2007–2007); Oklahoma City Thunder (2008–2015); Golden State Warriors (2016–2018); Brooklyn Nets (2020–2022); Phoenix Suns (2022–2024); Houston Rockets (2025–present).
