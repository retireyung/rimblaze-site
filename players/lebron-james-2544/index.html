LeBron James facts
How many career points does LeBron James have?

LeBron James has 43,537 career regular-season points in 1,628 games, 26.7 per game, which ranks 1st all-time.

How many triple-doubles does LeBron James have?

125 career triple-doubles and 625 double-doubles. The most recent was Mar 30, 2026 against WAS (21 points, 10 rebounds, 12 assists).

What is LeBron James’s career high?

61 points on Mar 3, 2014 against CHA.

What was LeBron James’s best season?

His best scoring season was 2005-06 with CLE: 31.4 points, 7.0 rebounds and 6.6 assists per game.

How is LeBron James doing this season?

2026-27: 16.2 points, 7.0 rebounds and 7.3 assists in 6 games, with 0 triple-doubles.

Where is LeBron James from?

LeBron James is from Akron, Ohio. He played high school ball at St. Vincent–St. Mary High School in Ohio, Ohio, then Buchtel High School in Ohio, Ohio. Before the NBA he played for St. Vincent-St. Mary HS (OH).

What teams has LeBron James played for?

Cleveland Cavaliers (2003–2009); Miami Heat (2010–2013); Cleveland Cavaliers (2014–2017); Los Angeles Lakers (2018–2025); Philadelphia 76ers (2026–present).
