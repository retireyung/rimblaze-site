Pau Gasol facts
How many career points did Pau Gasol score?

Pau Gasol scored 20,894 career regular-season points in 1,226 games, 17.0 per game, which ranks 44th all-time.

How many triple-doubles did Pau Gasol have?

10 career triple-doubles and 532 double-doubles. The most recent was Dec 23, 2017 against SAC (14 points, 11 rebounds, 10 assists).

What is Pau Gasol’s career high?

46 points on Jan 10, 2015 against MIL.

What was Pau Gasol’s best season?

His best scoring season was 2006-07 with MEM: 20.8 points, 9.8 rebounds and 3.4 assists per game.

Where is Pau Gasol from?

Pau Gasol is from Barcelona, Spain. He played college ball at University of Barcelona.

What teams did Pau Gasol play for?

Memphis Grizzlies (2001–2007); Los Angeles Lakers (2007–2013); Chicago Bulls (2014–2015); San Antonio Spurs (2016–2018); Milwaukee Bucks (2018–2018).
