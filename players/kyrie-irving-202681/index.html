Kyrie Irving facts
How many career points does Kyrie Irving have?

Kyrie Irving has 18,569 career regular-season points in 785 games, 23.7 per game, which ranks 74th all-time.

How many triple-doubles does Kyrie Irving have?

3 career triple-doubles and 72 double-doubles. The most recent was Nov 2, 2019 against DET (20 points, 11 rebounds, 10 assists).

What is Kyrie Irving’s career high?

How is Kyrie Irving doing this season?

Where is Kyrie Irving from?

Kyrie Irving is from Melbourne, Australia. He played high school ball at Montclair Kimberley Academy in New Jersey, New Jersey, then St. Patrick High School in Elizabeth, New Jersey. He played college ball at Duke University.

Cleveland Cavaliers (2011–2016); Boston Celtics (2017–2018); Brooklyn Nets (2019–2022); Dallas Mavericks (2022–2024); Dallas Mavericks (2026–present).
