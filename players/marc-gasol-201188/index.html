Marc Gasol facts
How many career points did Marc Gasol score?

Marc Gasol scored 12,514 career regular-season points in 891 games, 14.0 per game, which ranks 267th all-time.

How many triple-doubles did Marc Gasol have?

5 career triple-doubles and 198 double-doubles. The most recent was Jan 23, 2019 against CHA (22 points, 17 rebounds, 10 assists).

What is Marc Gasol’s career high?

42 points on Jan 25, 2017 against TOR.

What was Marc Gasol’s best season?

His best scoring season was 2016-17 with MEM: 19.5 points, 6.3 rebounds and 4.6 assists per game.

Where is Marc Gasol from?

Marc Gasol is from Barcelona, Spain. He played high school ball at Lausanne Collegiate School in Memphis, Tennessee. Before the NBA he played for FC Barcelona.

What teams did Marc Gasol play for?

Memphis Grizzlies (2008–2018); Toronto Raptors (2018–2019); Los Angeles Lakers (2020–2020).
