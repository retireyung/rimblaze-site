Rajon Rondo facts
How many career points did Rajon Rondo score?

Rajon Rondo scored 9,337 career regular-season points in 957 games, 9.8 per game, which ranks 461st all-time.

How many triple-doubles did Rajon Rondo have?

32 career triple-doubles and 237 double-doubles. The most recent was Mar 4, 2019 against LAC (24 points, 10 rebounds, 12 assists).

What is Rajon Rondo’s career high?

35 points on Feb 15, 2012 against DET.

What was Rajon Rondo’s best season?

His best scoring season was 2012-13 with BOS: 13.7 points, 5.6 rebounds and 11.1 assists per game.

Where is Rajon Rondo from?

Rajon Rondo is from Louisville, Kentucky. He played high school ball at Eastern High School in Kentucky, Kentucky. He played college ball at University of Kentucky.

What teams did Rajon Rondo play for?

Boston Celtics (2006–2014); Dallas Mavericks (2014–2014); Sacramento Kings (2015–2015); Chicago Bulls (2016–2016); New Orleans Pelicans (2017–2017); Los Angeles Lakers (2018–2019); Atlanta Hawks (2020–2020); LA Clippers (2020–2020); Los Angeles Lakers (2021–2021); Cleveland Cavaliers (2021–2021).
